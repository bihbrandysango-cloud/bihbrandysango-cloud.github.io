About

Sales first, then data

I'm a business intelligence analyst. I turn a company's sales, stock and invoicing records into Power BI reports the owner can read at a glance.

I started as a software engineer at CRTV Cameroon Radio Television, building Python and SQL tools on a five-person team. Then I moved to the business side. Since 2021 I have supported the CEO and Creative Director of Stanlion Clothing, a US fashion brand, and in 2026 I led business development for a law firm in Cameroon. Working next to sales is why my reports answer the questions sales teams ask.

At Stanlion I took on the company's sales, inventory and invoicing data. I wrote Python automation that cut manual data entry time by 40%, and built the SQL queries, Excel templates and Power BI dashboards we use to track it. As a freelance business intelligence analyst, I built a Power BI semantic model on 18 months of point-of-sale data for a US fashion retailer, so the owner can see what sells, who buys it and what to restock.

In October 2026 I completed the Women Techsters Fellowship (Tech4Dev) in data science and engineering.

Tools Power BI · SQL · Python · Excel

I'm open to business intelligence analyst roles and freelance BI projects. Send me a message.
